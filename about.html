About Us

A New Agency, Built on 7+ Years of Hands-On Experience

DsMonkTech Web Solution is headquartered in Akbarpur, Uttar Pradesh — but the agency's client work isn't limited to one city or one country. We build and optimize websites for businesses across India and worldwide, combining specialist SEO knowledge with fast, conversion-ready web development.

The brand itself is new. The person running it isn't new to the work: 7+ years designing and optimizing real websites for real businesses, across hospitality, food service, and retail. DsMonkTech was started to take that hands-on experience and offer it directly — without the account layers, junior hand-offs, or generic packages that slow down most agencies.

Every engagement is run by the person who actually does the SEO and the development. No outsourced guesswork, no diluted communication — just direct, accountable work.
